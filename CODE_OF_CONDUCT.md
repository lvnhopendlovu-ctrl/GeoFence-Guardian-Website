# Community code of conduct

GeoFence Guardian's public community should be useful, respectful, and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive and specific.
- Respect privacy and remove personal or location information.
- Welcome questions from people with different technical backgrounds.
- Correct mistakes with evidence and without personal attacks.
- Follow each platform's rules and applicable law.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct
- Publishing private information or precise device locations
- Requests to track someone without their knowledge or lawful authority
- Instructions intended to bypass device security or misuse the app
- Spam, fake engagement, impersonation, or deceptive promotion

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns through the [support page](https://geofenceguardians.co.za/support/) without repeating harmful or private content publicly.
